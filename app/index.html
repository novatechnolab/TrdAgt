🌅 Premarket (3:30 PM–9:00 AM)

Focus Areas:
  • ✓ Gap analysis from previous close
  • ✓ Pre-open OI accumulation/liquidation
  • ✓ Overnight news & events
  • ✓ FII/DII sentiment
  • ✓ Circuit breaker probability
Trade Setup:

Place orders at market open based on gap confirmation. Focus on breakouts with volume.

📊 Opening Bell (9:00–9:15 AM)

Focus Areas:
  • ✓ First 15-min momentum & range
  • ✓ Volume buildup in first candles
  • ✓ PCR ratio movement
  • ✓ IV expansion/contraction
  • ✓ Reversal or continuation signals
Trade Setup:

Enter options spreads (Bull Call/Put) when direction is confirmed. Use stop-loss at opening range.

⚡ Live Session (9:15 AM–3:30 PM)

Focus Areas:
  • ✓ Real-time price action & trends
  • ✓ PCR oscillations & max pain
  • ✓ IV crush/expansion dynamics
  • ✓ Intraday volume surges
  • ✓ Momentum confirmations (RSI, MACD)
Trade Setup:

Execute direct options buys or spreads based on live momentum. Manage exits near max pain levels.

📈 Quick Tips

  • 🎯 Max Pain: Unlikely direction (avoid going against it)
  • 💰 PCR Swing: >10% change = major direction shift
  • ⚡ IV Crush: Post-event, theta works in your favor
  • 📊 Volume Spike: >1.5x avg = strong conviction
  • 🔄 Reversal: Near max pain + opposite PCR = high prob
